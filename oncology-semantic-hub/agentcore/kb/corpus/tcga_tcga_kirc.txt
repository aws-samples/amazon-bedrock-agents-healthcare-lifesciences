TCGA project TCGA-KIRC: Kidney Renal Clear Cell Carcinoma.
Program: The Cancer Genome Atlas (TCGA).
Primary site(s): Kidney.
Disease type(s): Adenomas and Adenocarcinomas.
Cases: 537. Files: 34535.

This TCGA cohort (TCGA-KIRC) provides molecular and clinical data for Kidney Renal Clear Cell Carcinoma (primary site: Kidney; disease type: Adenomas and Adenocarcinomas). It supports biomarker, mutation, expression, and copy-number analyses in oncology research.
