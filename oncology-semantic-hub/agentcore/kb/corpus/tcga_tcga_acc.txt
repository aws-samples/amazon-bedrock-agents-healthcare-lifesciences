TCGA project TCGA-ACC: Adrenocortical Carcinoma.
Program: The Cancer Genome Atlas (TCGA).
Primary site(s): Adrenal gland.
Disease type(s): Adenomas and Adenocarcinomas.
Cases: 92. Files: 5789.

This TCGA cohort (TCGA-ACC) provides molecular and clinical data for Adrenocortical Carcinoma (primary site: Adrenal gland; disease type: Adenomas and Adenocarcinomas). It supports biomarker, mutation, expression, and copy-number analyses in oncology research.
