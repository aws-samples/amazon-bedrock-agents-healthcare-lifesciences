TCGA project TCGA-COAD: Colon Adenocarcinoma.
Program: The Cancer Genome Atlas (TCGA).
Primary site(s): Colon, Rectosigmoid junction.
Disease type(s): Cystic, Mucinous and Serous Neoplasms, Complex Epithelial Neoplasms, Epithelial Neoplasms, NOS, Adenomas and Adenocarcinomas.
Cases: 461. Files: 28900.

This TCGA cohort (TCGA-COAD) provides molecular and clinical data for Colon Adenocarcinoma (primary site: Colon, Rectosigmoid junction; disease type: Cystic, Mucinous and Serous Neoplasms, Complex Epithelial Neoplasms, Epithelial Neoplasms, NOS, Adenomas and Adenocarcinomas). It supports biomarker, mutation, expression, and copy-number analyses in oncology research.
