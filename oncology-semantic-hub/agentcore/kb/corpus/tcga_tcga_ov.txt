TCGA project TCGA-OV: Ovarian Serous Cystadenocarcinoma.
Program: The Cancer Genome Atlas (TCGA).
Primary site(s): Retroperitoneum and peritoneum, Ovary.
Disease type(s): Cystic, Mucinous and Serous Neoplasms, Not Reported.
Cases: 608. Files: 32864.

This TCGA cohort (TCGA-OV) provides molecular and clinical data for Ovarian Serous Cystadenocarcinoma (primary site: Retroperitoneum and peritoneum, Ovary; disease type: Cystic, Mucinous and Serous Neoplasms, Not Reported). It supports biomarker, mutation, expression, and copy-number analyses in oncology research.
