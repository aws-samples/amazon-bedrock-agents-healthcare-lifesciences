TCGA project TCGA-UCEC: Uterine Corpus Endometrial Carcinoma.
Program: The Cancer Genome Atlas (TCGA).
Primary site(s): Uterus, NOS, Corpus uteri.
Disease type(s): Not Reported, Cystic, Mucinous and Serous Neoplasms, Epithelial Neoplasms, NOS, Adenomas and Adenocarcinomas.
Cases: 560. Files: 33337.

This TCGA cohort (TCGA-UCEC) provides molecular and clinical data for Uterine Corpus Endometrial Carcinoma (primary site: Uterus, NOS, Corpus uteri; disease type: Not Reported, Cystic, Mucinous and Serous Neoplasms, Epithelial Neoplasms, NOS, Adenomas and Adenocarcinomas). It supports biomarker, mutation, expression, and copy-number analyses in oncology research.
