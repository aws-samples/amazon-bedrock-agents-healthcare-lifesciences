TCGA project TCGA-KIRP: Kidney Renal Papillary Cell Carcinoma.
Program: The Cancer Genome Atlas (TCGA).
Primary site(s): Kidney.
Disease type(s): Adenomas and Adenocarcinomas.
Cases: 291. Files: 18618.

This TCGA cohort (TCGA-KIRP) provides molecular and clinical data for Kidney Renal Papillary Cell Carcinoma (primary site: Kidney; disease type: Adenomas and Adenocarcinomas). It supports biomarker, mutation, expression, and copy-number analyses in oncology research.
