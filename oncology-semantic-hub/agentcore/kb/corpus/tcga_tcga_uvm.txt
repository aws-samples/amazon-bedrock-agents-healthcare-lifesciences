TCGA project TCGA-UVM: Uveal Melanoma.
Program: The Cancer Genome Atlas (TCGA).
Primary site(s): Eye and adnexa.
Disease type(s): Paragangliomas and Glomus Tumors, Nevi and Melanomas.
Cases: 80. Files: 5509.

This TCGA cohort (TCGA-UVM) provides molecular and clinical data for Uveal Melanoma (primary site: Eye and adnexa; disease type: Paragangliomas and Glomus Tumors, Nevi and Melanomas). It supports biomarker, mutation, expression, and copy-number analyses in oncology research.
