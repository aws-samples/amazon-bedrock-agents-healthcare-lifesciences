TCGA project TCGA-TGCT: Testicular Germ Cell Tumors.
Program: The Cancer Genome Atlas (TCGA).
Primary site(s): Testis.
Disease type(s): Germ Cell Neoplasms.
Cases: 263. Files: 12851.

This TCGA cohort (TCGA-TGCT) provides molecular and clinical data for Testicular Germ Cell Tumors (primary site: Testis; disease type: Germ Cell Neoplasms). It supports biomarker, mutation, expression, and copy-number analyses in oncology research.
