TCGA project TCGA-THCA: Thyroid Carcinoma.
Program: The Cancer Genome Atlas (TCGA).
Primary site(s): Thyroid gland.
Disease type(s): Squamous Cell Neoplasms, Epithelial Neoplasms, NOS, Adenomas and Adenocarcinomas.
Cases: 507. Files: 33411.

This TCGA cohort (TCGA-THCA) provides molecular and clinical data for Thyroid Carcinoma (primary site: Thyroid gland; disease type: Squamous Cell Neoplasms, Epithelial Neoplasms, NOS, Adenomas and Adenocarcinomas). It supports biomarker, mutation, expression, and copy-number analyses in oncology research.
