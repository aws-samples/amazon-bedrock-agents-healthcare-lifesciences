Program: The Cancer Genome Atlas (TCGA).

This TCGA cohort (TCGA-LGG) provides molecular and clinical data for Brain Lower Grade Glioma (primary site: Brain; disease type: Gliomas). It supports biomarker, mutation, expression, and copy-number analyses in oncology research.
