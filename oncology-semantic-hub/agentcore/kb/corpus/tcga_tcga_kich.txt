TCGA project TCGA-KICH: Kidney Chromophobe.
Program: The Cancer Genome Atlas (TCGA).
Primary site(s): Kidney.
Disease type(s): Adenomas and Adenocarcinomas.
Cases: 113. Files: 5993.

This TCGA cohort (TCGA-KICH) provides molecular and clinical data for Kidney Chromophobe (primary site: Kidney; disease type: Adenomas and Adenocarcinomas). It supports biomarker, mutation, expression, and copy-number analyses in oncology research.
