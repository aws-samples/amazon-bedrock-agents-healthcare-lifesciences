TCGA project TCGA-LAML: Acute Myeloid Leukemia.
Program: The Cancer Genome Atlas (TCGA).
Primary site(s): Hematopoietic and reticuloendothelial systems.
Disease type(s): Myeloid Leukemias.
Cases: 200. Files: 8839.

This TCGA cohort (TCGA-LAML) provides molecular and clinical data for Acute Myeloid Leukemia (primary site: Hematopoietic and reticuloendothelial systems; disease type: Myeloid Leukemias). It supports biomarker, mutation, expression, and copy-number analyses in oncology research.
