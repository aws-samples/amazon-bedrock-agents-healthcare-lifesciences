TCGA project TCGA-STAD: Stomach Adenocarcinoma.
Program: The Cancer Genome Atlas (TCGA).
Primary site(s): Stomach.
Disease type(s): Cystic, Mucinous and Serous Neoplasms, Adenomas and Adenocarcinomas.
Cases: 443. Files: 31088.

This TCGA cohort (TCGA-STAD) provides molecular and clinical data for Stomach Adenocarcinoma (primary site: Stomach; disease type: Cystic, Mucinous and Serous Neoplasms, Adenomas and Adenocarcinomas). It supports biomarker, mutation, expression, and copy-number analyses in oncology research.
