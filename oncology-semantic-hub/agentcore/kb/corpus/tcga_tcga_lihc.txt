TCGA project TCGA-LIHC: Liver Hepatocellular Carcinoma.
Program: The Cancer Genome Atlas (TCGA).
Primary site(s): Liver and intrahepatic bile ducts.
Disease type(s): Adenomas and Adenocarcinomas.
Cases: 377. Files: 23930.

This TCGA cohort (TCGA-LIHC) provides molecular and clinical data for Liver Hepatocellular Carcinoma (primary site: Liver and intrahepatic bile ducts; disease type: Adenomas and Adenocarcinomas). It supports biomarker, mutation, expression, and copy-number analyses in oncology research.
