TCGA project TCGA-CHOL: Cholangiocarcinoma.
Program: The Cancer Genome Atlas (TCGA).
Primary site(s): Liver and intrahepatic bile ducts, Other and unspecified parts of biliary tract, Pancreas.
Disease type(s): Adenomas and Adenocarcinomas.
Cases: 51. Files: 3171.

This TCGA cohort (TCGA-CHOL) provides molecular and clinical data for Cholangiocarcinoma (primary site: Liver and intrahepatic bile ducts, Other and unspecified parts of biliary tract, Pancreas; disease type: Adenomas and Adenocarcinomas). It supports biomarker, mutation, expression, and copy-number analyses in oncology research.
