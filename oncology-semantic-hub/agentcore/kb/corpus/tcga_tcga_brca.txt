Program: The Cancer Genome Atlas (TCGA).
Primary site(s): Breast.
Disease type(s): Adnexal and Skin Appendage Neoplasms, Adenomas and Adenocarcinomas, Ductal and Lobular Neoplasms, Cystic, Mucinous and Serous Neoplasms, Complex Epithelial Neoplasms, Fibroepithelial Neoplasms, Squamous Cell Neoplasms, Epithelial Neoplasms, NOS, Basal Cell Neoplasms.
Cases: 1098. Files: 70774.

This TCGA cohort (TCGA-BRCA) provides molecular and clinical data for Breast Invasive Carcinoma (primary site: Breast; disease type: Adnexal and Skin Appendage Neoplasms, Adenomas and Adenocarcinomas, Ductal and Lobular Neoplasms, Cystic, Mucinous and Serous Neoplasms, Complex Epithelial Neoplasms, Fibroepithelial Neoplasms, Squamous Cell Neoplasms, Epithelial Neoplasms, NOS, Basal Cell Neoplasms). It supports biomarker, mutation, expression, and copy-number analyses in oncology research.
