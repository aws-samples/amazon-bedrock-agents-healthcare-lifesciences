TCGA project TCGA-GBM: Glioblastoma Multiforme.
Program: The Cancer Genome Atlas (TCGA).
Primary site(s): Brain.
Disease type(s): Not Reported, Gliomas.
Cases: 617. Files: 30326.

This TCGA cohort (TCGA-GBM) provides molecular and clinical data for Glioblastoma Multiforme (primary site: Brain; disease type: Not Reported, Gliomas). It supports biomarker, mutation, expression, and copy-number analyses in oncology research.
