TCGA project TCGA-PRAD: Prostate Adenocarcinoma.
Program: The Cancer Genome Atlas (TCGA).
Primary site(s): Prostate gland.
Disease type(s): Ductal and Lobular Neoplasms, Cystic, Mucinous and Serous Neoplasms, Acinar Cell Neoplasms, Adenomas and Adenocarcinomas.
Cases: 500. Files: 31915.

This TCGA cohort (TCGA-PRAD) provides molecular and clinical data for Prostate Adenocarcinoma (primary site: Prostate gland; disease type: Ductal and Lobular Neoplasms, Cystic, Mucinous and Serous Neoplasms, Acinar Cell Neoplasms, Adenomas and Adenocarcinomas). It supports biomarker, mutation, expression, and copy-number analyses in oncology research.
