TCGA project TCGA-LUAD: Lung Adenocarcinoma.
Program: The Cancer Genome Atlas (TCGA).
Primary site(s): Bronchus and lung.
Disease type(s): Ductal and Lobular Neoplasms, Cystic, Mucinous and Serous Neoplasms, Acinar Cell Neoplasms, Adenomas and Adenocarcinomas.
Cases: 585. Files: 36224.

This TCGA cohort (TCGA-LUAD) provides molecular and clinical data for Lung Adenocarcinoma (primary site: Bronchus and lung; disease type: Ductal and Lobular Neoplasms, Cystic, Mucinous and Serous Neoplasms, Acinar Cell Neoplasms, Adenomas and Adenocarcinomas). It supports biomarker, mutation, expression, and copy-number analyses in oncology research.
