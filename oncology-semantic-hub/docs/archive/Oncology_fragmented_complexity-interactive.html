🛡️ Governance is not an afterthought

The semantic hub doesn't just connect data — it governs access, tracks provenance, and ensures trust.

Data lineage & provenance Ontology versioning Access control (ABAC) Audit trail Quality scoring HITL curation flywheel

"Agents interact through the semantic layer — never bypassing to raw data. Zero ungoverned facts reach the knowledge graph."

🤖 Why now: Agentic AI needs context

AI agents can automate 75–85% of pharma workflows — but only if they have the semantic context to reason accurately.

Multi-hop graph reasoning GraphRAG (not just RAG) Schema discovery at runtime Explainable traversal paths Facts, not hallucinations Cross-domain tool orchestration

"Without the semantic layer, agents lack the context to reason accurately. The hub is the prerequisite for Stages 4–6 of the research maturity model."
